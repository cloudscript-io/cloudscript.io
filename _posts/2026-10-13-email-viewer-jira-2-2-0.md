Version 2.2.0 is a copy correction with no behaviour, permission or module change. The pointer line at the foot of the Attached emails group now reads "Read it in Emails, in the issue's Activity section." (or "Read them in Emails, in the issue's Activity section."), replacing "…in the Emails tab.", so that it is true in Jira's classic view, in the new work item view that Jira is rolling out from 19 October 2026, and in Jira Service Management. Details of where the tab and the group sit in each view are in the [user guide](/apps/email-viewer-jira).
