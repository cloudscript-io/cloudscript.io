Support

Need a hand with one of our Confluence Cloud or Jira Cloud apps? Most answers are in the product guides in our help centre. If you still need us, raise a request there or email support@cloudscript.io, and a real person will help you sort it out.

Open the help centre Email support

Or write to us directly at support@cloudscript.io.

What to include

To help us answer quickly, it's useful to tell us which app you're using (for example CloudScript Mermaid Diagrams for Confluence), what you were trying to do, and what happened instead. A screenshot or the diagram code often helps.

Response times

We're a small team and answer every message ourselves. We aim to reply within a couple of business days; complex issues may take a little longer to investigate.

Privacy

Support requests, whether raised in the help centre or sent by email, are handled in Atlassian's customer service platform, and we use what you send us only to respond to your request. The help centre uses Atlassian's cookies, which are separate from this website's cookie-free page counter; our Trust Center explains which cookies are set and where your request is stored. Please never send passwords, API tokens or other credentials. See the Trust Center too for the privacy policy that applies to the app you're using.
